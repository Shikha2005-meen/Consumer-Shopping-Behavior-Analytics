8. POTENTIAL BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Product strategy: Review category-level purchase amounts and record counts before deciding inventory or marketing priorities.
2. Seasonal planning: Compare seasonal patterns when planning campaigns, but validate them with additional time-based data.
3. Promotions: Compare discount groups, while avoiding claims that discounts caused higher spending.
4. Customer engagement: Compare subscription groups and investigate whether observed differences are meaningful.
5. Customer experience: Review rating distributions and investigate the reasons behind low ratings before proposing improvements.

Important limitation: These are observational dataset findings. Purchase amount is not profit, and group differences do not establish causation. Recommendations require validation before implementation.
